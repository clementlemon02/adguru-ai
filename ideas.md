**Direction A: "Dark Studio" — Cinematic Post-Production Aesthetic**
- **Design Movement:** Brutalist-meets-Cinema UI (inspired by professional video editing suites like DaVinci Resolve)
- **Core Principles:** Dark, high-contrast surfaces; orange/amber accent as the "record" signal; monospaced type for data; editorial asymmetry
- **Color Philosophy:** Near-black charcoal (#0D0D0F) background, warm amber (#F59E0B) as the primary action color (evoking a recording light), off-white (#F5F0E8) for body text. The amber represents the "on-air" signal — the AI is always watching.
- **Layout Paradigm:** Horizontal split panels. Left side carries the narrative text; right side shows the visual demo/mockup. Sections break with diagonal cuts (clip-path), not horizontal lines.
- **Signature Elements:** Waveform-style decorative lines as section dividers; circular "record" button motifs; video timecode typography (00:00:00)
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Elements "scan in" from left like a video timeline. Hover states reveal a red "REC" dot.
- **Animation:** Staggered fade-in on scroll; waveform animations on the hero; subtle scanline overlay on video mockups
- **Typography System:** `Space Grotesk` (bold display) + `JetBrains Mono` (data/timecode) + `Inter` (body)

**Direction B: "Signal Intelligence" — Data-Driven Analytical Aesthetic**
- **Design Movement:** Bloomberg Terminal meets Figma — analytical precision with modern clarity
- **Core Principles:** Information density; grid-based data visualization; electric blue on white; every element earns its place
- **Color Philosophy:** Crisp white (#FFFFFF) background, electric blue (#2563EB) as primary, slate (#1E293B) for headings. Accent with a sharp coral (#F43F5E) for "problem" indicators. The palette signals intelligence and precision.

- **Signature Elements:** Score gauges and radial charts; annotation callout boxes (like Figma comments); a live "processing" terminal window
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Hover over any metric to see its definition. The demo section is interactive — users can click through the critique steps.
- **Animation:** Counter animations on stats; progress bars that fill on scroll; subtle grid-line background
- **Typography System:** `DM Sans` (headings) + `IBM Plex Mono` (metrics/code) + `DM Sans` (body)

**Direction C: "The Guru" — Bold Editorial Magazine Aesthetic**
- **Design Movement:** Kinetic Typography meets Editorial Magazine (inspired by Wired, Pentagram)
- **Core Principles:** Oversized typography as a visual element; high contrast black and white with a single electric accent; confident, opinionated layout
- **Color Philosophy:** Off-white parchment (#FAF7F2) background, near-black (#111111) for type, electric lime (#A3E635) as the sole accent color. The lime is unexpected and memorable — it signals "new thinking."
- **Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed typographic sections. The hero is almost entirely type — a massive headline that takes up 80% of the viewport. Images are used sparingly but powerfully.
- **Signature Elements:** Large rotated text labels; bold horizontal rules; pull-quote style callouts with oversized quotation marks
- **Interaction Philosophy:** Scroll-triggered text reveals; words that animate in one by one; the CTA button has a magnetic hover effect
- **Animation:** Kinetic text entrance (words slide up); parallax on the hero; smooth section transitions
- **Typography System:** `Bebas Neue` (display/hero) + `Playfair Display` (subheadings) + `Source Serif 4` (body)

## Selected Direction: A — "Dark Studio"

The "Dark Studio" aesthetic is the strongest fit for AdGuru AI because:
1. It visually communicates the product's domain (video production, marketing)
2. The dark background makes the demo mockups and annotation circles pop visually
3. The amber "record" accent creates a memorable, distinctive brand identity
4. It feels premium and trustworthy to both investors and performance marketers
